socket_activation.c for UNIX and IPv4 stream sockets:

    Parsing .socket units from loaded units

    Handling ListenStream= values for filesystem UNIX sockets and IPv4 host:port TCP sockets

    Creating sockets with SOCK_NONBLOCK and SOCK_CLOEXEC

    Binding UNIX sockets to filesystem paths and IPv4 sockets to configured host/port endpoints

    Adding sockets to the sd-event loop with readable event callbacks

    Accepting incoming connections and activating the matching service as a placeholder until descriptor passing is implemented

    Cleaning up UNIX socket paths and listener descriptors during daemon shutdown
